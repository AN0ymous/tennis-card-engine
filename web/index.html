        Known limits

        • eBay only. Fanatics Collect and Alt have no public search, so check those by hand.
        • eBay hands over the newest 1,200 listings per search, so an older listing turns up only through a player scan.
        • A seller's title can state the wrong serial. Cards with any doubt are marked Check by eye; always look at the photo.
        • Prices and sold statuses are as fresh as the last daily scan.
